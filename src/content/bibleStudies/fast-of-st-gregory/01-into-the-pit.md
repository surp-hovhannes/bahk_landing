## Opening Prayer

Lord God, who did not abandon Your servant Gregory in the pit of Khor Virap, be with us in our own dark seasons. Teach us that silence is not Your absence, and that hidden places are not forsaken places. As You drew him up and set his feet upon a rock, draw us from fear, doubt, and grief, and put a new song in our mouths. Amen.

## Scripture Reading

**Psalm 40:1–3** — *"I waited patiently for the Lord; he inclined to me and heard my cry. He drew me up from the desolate pit, out of the miry bog, and set my feet upon a rock, making my steps secure. He put a new song in my mouth, a song of praise to our God. Many will see and fear, and put their trust in the Lord."*

## Reflection

For thirteen years, St. Gregory the Illuminator was imprisoned in the pit of Khor Virap by King Tiridates — not for any crime, but for his fidelity to Christ. The Armenian Church does not read this as simple suffering or punishment, but as a holy transformation. The darkness of the pit became a womb of preparation, a place where Gregory's prayers rose silently to God and where the Spirit, rather than abandoning him, was quietly shaping him for his purpose. Psalm 40 echoes this experience: waiting patiently, crying out, and trusting that God will draw us up and set our feet on solid ground. The devotional invites us to look at the "pits" in our own lives — seasons of grief, illness, failure, or anxiety — not as signs of God's absence, but as hidden places of formation.

## Group Discussion Questions

Gather with your group and discuss:

1. The devotional invites us to see Gregory's thirteen years in the pit not as punishment but as preparation. Have you had a season in your own life that felt like a pit — and looking back, can you see how God may have been at work in it?

2. The Psalm says "I waited patiently for the Lord" — but waiting patiently is rarely easy. What makes it hard for you to trust God during long seasons of silence or darkness?

3. St. Gregory's only crime was fidelity to Christ. Is there a way your own faithfulness to God has ever cost you something — socially, professionally, or personally? What did that feel like?

## Personal Reflection Questions

Take some quiet time with these:

1. The devotional says, "Perhaps God was not punishing you, but preparing you." Is there a difficulty in your life right now that you could begin to see differently through that lens?

2. Pray the prayer offered in the devotional: *"Lord, in my own dark seasons, remind me that you are near. That the silence is not absence. Prepare me in the hidden places. Shape my soul for your purpose. Amen."* Sit with it. What word or phrase stays with you?

## Closing Prayer

Holy Spirit, who formed Gregory in darkness before he shone in the light, form us in the hidden places of this fast. Where the world cannot see, keep us from discouragement; where we cannot see, give us patience. Let the songs we learn in the pit become the witness we carry into the day. Seal in our hearts what we have heard and shared. Amen.
